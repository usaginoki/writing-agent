# L08 Global Coherence

**Summary:** Coherence is an experience readers build from signals. Extend the introduction principle to every unit: open each document, section, subsection, paragraph (and sentence) with a short, graspable segment ending in a point sentence that names the key themes; repeat those themes in the longer body. Also make everything relevant to the point and ordered in a visible way (chronological, coordinate, logical). Spend effort on beginnings. The patterns may seem formulaic but serve readers who want to understand.

## Principles
- [[Open every unit with a short framing segment]]
- [[Put the point sentence at the end of the opening segment]]
- [[Name key themes toward the end of the point sentence]]
- [[Repeat key themes in the body]]
- [[Make every sentence relevant to the point]]
- [[Order parts chronologically, coordinately, or logically and signal it]]
- [[Do not narrate your thinking]]
- [[Mark where introductions and sections begin and end]]
- [[Promise a withheld point at the end of the introduction]]
- [[Spend revision effort on beginnings]]
- [[Introduce sections clearly so imperfect paragraphs survive]]
- [[Accept formulaic structure for clarity]]

## Summing up (condensed)
Open each unit with a relatively short segment; end it with the point sentence; use key themes toward its end; in the longer segment use consistent topics, repeat key terms, follow old-new order, order parts understandably, keep every sentence relevant.

## Quotes
> "Coherence is an experience we create for ourselves as we make our own sense out of what we read."
> "Get beginnings straight, and the rest is likely to take care of itself."
