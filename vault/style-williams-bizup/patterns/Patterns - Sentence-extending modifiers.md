# Patterns - sentence-extending modifiers

| Device | Recipe | Tacked-on (✗) | Extended (✓) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Resumptive Modifier]] (noun) | noun + comma + repeat noun + *that* clause | …what I have not done in this sentence, which I could have ended… | …what I am about to do in this sentence, a sentence that I could have ended at that comma… |
| Resumptive (adjective) | repeat adjective | — | …a voice that was both true and lyrical, true to the rhythms of the working man's speech and lyrical in its celebration of his labor. |
| Resumptive (verb) | repeat verb | — | All who value independence should resist the trivialization of government regulation, resist its obsession with administrative tidiness… |
| Resumptive (*one that*) | *one that* | — | …a problem we have wholly ignored, one that has plagued societies… |
| Resumptive (noun, summing up) | repeat noun/idea | When we discovered the earth was not the center of the universe, it changed our understanding of who we are | …, an understanding changed again by Darwin, again by Freud, and again by Einstein. |
| [[Summative Modifier]] | complete clause + comma + summing noun + *that* | …less than zero, which will have serious social implications. | …less than zero, a demographic event that will have serious social implications. |
| Summative | | | American productivity has risen to new heights, an achievement that only a decade ago was considered an impossible dream. |
| [[Free Modifier]] (-ing) | clause + comma + *-ing* phrase on the subject | — | Free modifiers resemble resumptive and summative modifiers, letting you extend the line of a sentence… |
| Free (past participle) | *-ed* phrase | — | Leonardo da Vinci was a man of powerful intellect, driven by an insatiable curiosity and haunted by a vision of artistic perfection. |
| Free (adjective) | adjective phrase | — | In 1939, we began to assist the British against Germany, aware that we faced another world war. |
| Free (movable) | can open the sentence | — | Driven by an insatiable curiosity, Leonardo da Vinci was… / Aware that we faced another world war, in 1939 we began… |
| Free (-ing) | | | Global warming will become a central political issue…, raising questions whose answers will affect the standard of living in every Western nation. |

**Practice frame (Ex. 10.2):** Within ten years, we could meet our energy needs with solar power, **needs** that will soar as our population grows (resumptive) / **a possibility** that few anticipated (summative) / **freeing** ourselves of dependence on foreign oil (free).
