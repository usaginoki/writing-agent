# Patterns - Noun string unpacking

Recipe: number words left to right; start with the last noun (usually a nominalization) as a verb; reorder; add prepositions; keep field terms.

| Before | Order | After |
|---|---|---|
| early childhood thought disorder misdiagnosis | 5-4-3-1-2 | misdiagnose disordered thought in young children |
| (keep technical term) | 5-(3,4)-1-2 | misdiagnose thought disorders in young children |
| board candidate review meeting schedule | 5-4-3-2-1 | schedule of meetings to review candidates for the board |

Fine as is: stone wall, student center, space shuttle.
