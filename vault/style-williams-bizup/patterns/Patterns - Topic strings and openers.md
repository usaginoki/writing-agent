# Patterns - Topic strings and openers

| Problem | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Placeholder subject hides topic | It is impossible for your claim to be proved. | (topic first) Your claim cannot be proved. |
| Topic buried in prepositional phrase | In regard to this question, I believe more research is needed. | This question needs more research. |
| Abstract subjects, random shifts | Consistent ideas toward the beginnings of sentences... A sense of coherence arises... | Readers understand... when they see... They feel a passage is coherent when they read... |
| Throat-clearing | And, therefore, it is important to note that, in Eastern states since 1980, acid rain has become a serious problem. | Since 1980, therefore, acid rain has become a serious problem in the Eastern states. |
| Over-repeated topic | "Moral climate" is... Moral climate... moral climate... | "Moral climate" is... This climate... morality... A moral climate... |

Steps: underline openers ([[Topic string test]]) > pick 2-3 topics > make them subjects > trim pre-subject words > vary a few words only if repetition is exact.
