# Patterns - Redundant pairs and modifiers

Keep one of each pair; delete the modifier its word implies. Principles: [[Delete redundant pairs]], [[Delete redundant modifiers]].

## Redundant pairs (keep one)
| pair | pair | pair |
|---|---|---|
| full and complete | hope and trust | any and all |
| true and accurate | each and every | basic and fundamental |
| hopes and desires | first and foremost | various and sundry |
| (ex.) final aims and goals | ready and willing | aid and assistance |

## Redundant modifiers (delete the italic/implied one)
| terrible tragedy | various different | free gift |
|---|---|---|
| basic fundamentals | future plans | each individual |
| final outcome | true facts | consensus of opinion |
| (ex.) past history | completely revolutionize | unexpectedly surprises |

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| If and when we can define our final aims and goals, each and every member ... ready and willing to offer aid and assistance | If we define our goals, we will all be ready to help |
| official governmental red tape seriously destroys initiative among individual businesses | Government red tape destroys business initiative |
