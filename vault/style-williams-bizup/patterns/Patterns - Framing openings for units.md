# Patterns - Framing openings for units

Model: opening segment (short) ending in point + key themes > longer segment with consistent topics, repeated key terms, old-new order, relevance, sensible order.

| Level | Opening segment | Before > after |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence | subject/topic; main clause states point | "Resistance in Nevada... has been heated" > "Nevada has heatedly resisted its use as a waste disposal site" |
| Complex sentence | main clause first = point | "Greater knowledge... has led to a reassessment" > "Historians are reassessing Columbus' role..., because they know more..." |
| Paragraph | 1-2 sentences; point last; themes late | background-first study paragraph > "In this study... successfully... inconsistent and less than predicted... no measurable effect." |
| Section | a paragraph or more; point at end | -- |
| Document | introduction of one or more paragraphs | -- |

Alzheimer's model: *Researchers have made strides in the early and accurate diagnosis of Alzheimer's, but those diagnoses have raised a new human problem about informing those at risk...* (point with themes) > body repeats *diagnosis, accuracy, predict, human tragedy*.
