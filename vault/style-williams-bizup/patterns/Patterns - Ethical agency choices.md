# Patterns - Ethical agency choices

| Evasion pattern (before) | Honest pattern (after) | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| Mistakes may have occurred (intransitive verb) | We made mistakes | [[Do not hide the responsible agent to deflect blame]] |
| Mistakes may have been made (agentless passive) | We made mistakes | same |
| The restructuring of rates is consistent with policy... (abstract subject) | We can now make you pay more | [[Name the affected reader and the real actor in high-stakes text]] |
| A defect... may exist on your vehicle; its failure could affect directional control (parts as subjects, nominalized harm) | If you brake hard and the plate fails, you could crash | same |
| Necessity constrains them to alter their governments (abstraction coerces) | We must alter our governments | [[Notice when grammar argues for you]] |
| He has refused / has forbidden (agent as subject) vs. Assent has not been forthcoming (hides agent) | choose the one that matches the claim you will defend | same |
| Shiites and Sunnis distrust because they have fought (agents) vs. Generations of conflict have caused distrust (circumstances) | pick knowingly: free choice or circumstance? | same |
| I'm afraid our funding didn't come through | acceptable kind indirectness | [[Distinguish kind softening from deceptive indirectness]] |
