# Patterns - Generic pronoun options

| Option | Example | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| generic *he* | Everyone realizes that he must answer for his actions. | biased |
| *he or she* | his or her readers | clumsy when repeated |
| *he/she*, *s/he* | s/he may respond | worse |
| plural noun + *they* | When writers ignore their readers' ethnicity, they ... | *they* may refer to writers or readers (ambiguous) |
| singular noun repeated | When a writer ignores his reader's ethnicity, his reader ... | precise but gendered |
| first person *we* | If we ignore the ethnicity of our readers ... | *we* may be ambiguous |
| impersonal abstraction | Failure to consider ethnicity may lead to ... | nominalized, vague |
| alternate *he*/*she* | (author's own practice) | intrusive/patronizing to some |
| singular *they* | No one should turn in their writing unedited. | some call it lazy; likely future standard |

Method: pick the option that keeps referents clear for your audience; no option is perfect.
