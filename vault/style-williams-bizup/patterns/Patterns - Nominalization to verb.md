# Patterns - Nominalization to verb

Five recurring shapes (p. 36-37) plus conversion list. Always: nominalization -> verb; character -> subject.

| # | Shape | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nominalization is subject of empty verb (`be, seem, has`) | The intention of the committee is to audit the records. | The committee intends to audit the records. |
| 2 | Nominalization follows empty verb | The agency conducted an investigation into the matter. | The agency investigated the matter. |
| 3 | Subject nominalization + empty verb + second nominalization | Our loss in sales was a result of their expansion of outlets. | We lost sales because they expanded outlets. |
| 4 | Nominalization after `there is/are` | There is no need for our further study of this problem. | We need not study this problem further. |
| 5 | Two-three nominalizations joined by prepositions | We did a review of the evolution of the brain. | First, we reviewed the evolution of the brain. / ...how the brain evolved. |

## Steps per pattern
- 1: verbify; find character; make subject.
- 2: verbify; replace empty verb.
- 3: verbify both; characters as subjects; link with connector ([[Patterns - Clause connectors]]).
- 4: verbify; character as subject.
- 5: verbify the first; leave second or recast as a `how/why` clause.

## Common conversions
| Verb -> noun | Adjective -> noun | Gerund | Same form |
|---|---|---|---|
| discover -> discovery | careless -> carelessness | fly -> flying | hope, result, repair, request, review |
| resist -> resistance | different -> difference | sing -> singing | cause (`causes` / `a cause`) |
| react -> reaction | proficient -> proficiency | | |

Actions also hide in adjectives (`applicable` -> applies; `indicative`, `dubious`, `argumentative`, `deserving`).
