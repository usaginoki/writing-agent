# Patterns - Hidden characters

| Where character hides | Before | Character found | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective | governmental intervention | government | a government intervenes |
| Adjective | theological debates / philosophical thought | theologians / philosophers | theologians debated / philosophers consider |
| Possessive | the Wolf's jump | the Wolf | the Wolf jumped |
| `of` object | distortion of market evolution | markets | how markets evolve |
| `by` object | outsourcing by corporations | corporations | corporations outsource |
| Absent | A decision was made in favor of doing a study | invent: we/I/researchers | We decided that... |
| Absent (general) | the presence of psychopathology is dependent... | researchers / one / we | choose a general term, then `we` |
