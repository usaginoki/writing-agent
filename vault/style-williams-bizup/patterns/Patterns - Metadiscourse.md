# Patterns - Metadiscourse

See [[Metadiscourse]]. Principles: [[Cut metadiscourse that attributes ideas to a source]], [[Cut metadiscourse that announces your topic]], [[Work topic phrases into the subject]].

## Three kinds (from Lesson 4)
| refers to | examples |
|---|---|
| writer's intentions | to sum up, candidly, I believe |
| directions to reader | note that, consider now, as you see |
| text structure | first, second, finally, therefore, however |

## Usually cut
| type | marker | ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|
| attribution | have been observed / determined to have | High divorce rates have been observed to occur in areas that have been determined to have low population density | High divorce rates occur in areas with low population density |
| topic announcement | This section introduces...; The first thing to say about it is | This section introduces another problem, that of noise pollution. The first thing to say... | Another problem is noise pollution. First, it exists... |
| topic flag | In regard to X, ... | In regard to a vigorous style, the most important feature is... | The most important feature of a vigorous style is... |
| topic flag | So far as X is concerned, ... | So far as China's industrial development is concerned, it has long surpassed that of Japan | China has long surpassed Japan's industrial development |
| stacked | The last point I would like to make is that in regard to ... it is important to keep in mind that | (34 words, 9 on subject) | The greatest changes in men-women relationships have occurred in how they work together |
| in-solution | In this study, I examine... | | Congress has legislated to protect children in the workplace |

## Keep
Structural words (*first, second, therefore, on the other hand*) and hedging words; some metadiscourse is needed in everything.
