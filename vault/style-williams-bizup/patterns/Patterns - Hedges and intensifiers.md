# Patterns - Hedges and intensifiers

Principles: [[Calibrate hedges]], [[Calibrate intensifiers]]. Concepts: [[Hedge]], [[Intensifier]].

## Hedges
| class | items |
|---|---|
| Adverbs | usually, often, sometimes, almost, virtually, possibly, allegedly, arguably, perhaps, apparently, in some ways, to a certain extent, somewhat, in some/certain respects |
| Adjectives | most, many, some, a certain number of |
| Verbs | may, might, can, could, seem, tend, appear, suggest, indicate |

## Intensifiers
| class | items |
|---|---|
| Adverbs | very, pretty, quite, rather, clearly, obviously, undoubtedly, certainly, of course, indeed, inevitably, invariably, always, literally |
| Adjectives | key, central, crucial, basic, fundamental, major, principal, essential |
| Verbs/phrases | show, prove, establish, as you/we/everyone knows/can see, it is clear/obvious that |

Most common intensifier: **absence of a hedge**.

## Calibration ladder
| level | example |
|---|---|
| too uncertain | There seems to be some evidence to suggest that certain differences ... could derive from ... possibly traceable to... |
| right | This evidence suggests that aspects of Japanese and Western rhetoric differ because... |
| too certain | This evidence proves that Japanese and Western rhetorics differ because... |
| too certain (gun) | I prove that... | 
| too uncertain (gun) | Some of my recent research seems to imply that there may be a risk that certain people ... could be more prone... |
| just right (gun) | My research indicates that people with a gun in their homes are more likely to use it to kill themselves or a family member than... |
| aggressive absolutes | all liberals; any censorship; every court; completely persuasive; totally a thing of the past |
| moderate | many liberals; most courts; persuasive enough; few people may remember; virtually a thing of the past |

*suggest* and *indicate* = claim you are confident enough to propose though less than 100% certain.
