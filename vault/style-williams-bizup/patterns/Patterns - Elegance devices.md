# Patterns - Elegance devices

Use sparingly, at key moments (see [[Use elegant devices sparingly]]).

| Device | Shape | Example | Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coordinated balance | A and B / C and D, parts echo in order, sound, meaning, later parts longer | ...for the administration to be arbitrary and for the opposition to be revolutionary and irreconcilable | [[Balance coordinated parts of a sentence]] |
| Uncoordinated balance | subject vs. object; subordinate vs. main clause | Were I trading scholarly principles for financial security, I would not be writing short books on minor subjects for small audiences | [[Balance uncoordinated parts of a sentence]] |
| *Not only . . . but also* frame | opener that forces a balanced second half | Picasso was not only a master draftsman... but also . . . | [[Balance coordinated parts of a sentence]] |
| Weighty ending | final noun or noun pair, never a preposition | ...the rescue and the liberation of the old | [[End sentences on weighty words]] |
| *Of* + weighty word | light *of the* + stressed noun | the fairest part of the earth, the most civilized portion of mankind | [[End with of plus a weighty word]] |
| Echoing salience | final stressed word echoes earlier one | style shapes, and in turn is shaped by, substance | [[Echo an earlier word at the sentence end]] |
| Chiasmus | 1A1B : 2B2A | our own thinking... the understanding of our readers | [[Use chiasmus for special effect]] |
| Suspension | parallel *if* clauses, longest member last | If journalists... if they... they would make journalism more useful... | [[Suspend the climax with parallel openers]] |
| Short sentences | under ~15 words, repeated | Trotsky, Pound | [[Vary sentence length only at the extremes]] |
| Very long sentence | coordinated free + resumptive modifiers | Mailer | [[Vary sentence length only at the extremes]] |
| Extravagant combination | all of the above, plus resumptive and summative modifiers | Oates, Turner | [[Use elegant devices sparingly]] |

## Rewrite shapes
- ✗ flat string: executive governmental powers -> ✓ the executive powers of government
- ✗ ends on preposition: give support to -> ✓ support
- ✗ A, B, C order repeated -> ✓ A, B, C, then mirror C, B, A (chiasmus; rare)
