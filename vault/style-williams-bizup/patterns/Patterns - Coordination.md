# Patterns - coordination

| Issue / device | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sprawl → coordination | which…which…that chain → parallel items | the minor, the unfinished, or even the botched work; should—and should not—be done; provide… and which can lead to… (Hoffman) |
| Short to long | long-short → short-long (+ parallel to lengthen) | ✗ those subjective values that reflect our deepest ethical choices and objective research → ✓ objective research and those subjective values that reflect our deepest ethical choices and strongest intellectual commitments |
| Correlatives | both X and Y; not only X but Y; (n)either X (n)or Y | Great Britain is both a good trading partner and a reliable ally… |
| Correlative misplaced | verb vs. subject after *but* | ✗ not only help… but you enhance → ✓ not only help… but enhance |
| Mismatched grammar | gerund + that-clause | ✗ revising the curriculum… and that the division be reorganized → ✓ that X be revised… and that Y be reorganized / revising… and reorganizing… |
| Accepted nonparallel | noun phrase + *how* clause | the problems of education in developing nations and how coordinated efforts can address them |
| Accepted nonparallel | adverb + prepositional phrase | quickly, carefully, and with the help of many |
| Bare *and* | and… and… → real relations | ✗ …, and it leads to… and will not be solved → ✓ …because it devalues… but it will not be solved… |
| Lost connection | long first half | shorten first half / repeat reminder word (*to remember*) / repeat noun (*students*) |
| Parallel for sense (summing up) | ✗ Besides the fact that no civilization has experienced such rapid alterations in their spiritual and mental lives, the material conditions… → ✓ No civilization has experienced such rapid alterations in their spiritual and mental lives and in the material conditions of daily existence. |
