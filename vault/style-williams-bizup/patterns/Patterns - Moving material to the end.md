# Patterns - Moving material to the end

| Device | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Trim the end | ...control our social behavior in the way we act in situations we are in every day. | ...control our social behavior. |
| Peripheral left (attribution) | ...would disappear, according to most atmospheric scientists. | According to most atmospheric scientists, ...would disappear. |
| Peripheral left (hedge) | The data ... are weak, for the most part. | For the most part, the data ... are weak. |
| Metadiscourse left | ...are getting scarcer, it must be remembered. | It must be remembered that ...are getting scarcer. |
| New right (inversion) | Questions about the ethics ... are more difficult. | More difficult are questions about the ethics... |
| There shift | Several syntactic devices let you... | There are several syntactic devices that let you... |
| Passive | ...our genes influence aspects of behavior... | ...aspects of behavior ... are influenced by our genes. |
| What shift | We need a monetary policy that... | What we need is a monetary policy that... |
| It shift | That oil prices would be set by OPEC once seemed inevitable. | It once seemed inevitable that oil prices would be set by OPEC. |
| Not only-but | ...clarify these issues and develop trust. | ...not only clarify these issues, but also develop trust. |
| Pronoun/ellipsis | ...but more often you cannot represent it in that kind of sentence. | ...but more often you cannot. |
