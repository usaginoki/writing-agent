# Patterns - Redundant categories

Cut the general noun naming the category. Principle: [[Delete redundant categories]].

## General nouns used redundantly
| large in **size** | round in **shape** | honest in **character** |
|---|---|---|
| unusual in **nature** | of a strange **type** | **area** of mathematics |
| of a bright **color** | at an early **time** | in a confused **state** |

## Transformations
| shape | ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|---|
| delete the category | period of time; membrane area; pink in color; shiny in appearance | period; membrane; pink; shiny |
| adjective + manner to adverb | aligned in an accurate manner | aligned accurately |
| adjective + system/activities to noun | educational system and public recreational activities | education and public recreation |
| frame "in the area of X" | In the area of education, tight financial conditions ... | Tight finances ... |
