# Patterns - Negative to affirmative

Principles: [[Change negatives to affirmatives]], [[Untangle stacked negatives]].

## Direct swaps
| negative | affirmative | negative | affirmative |
|---|---|---|---|
| not careful | careless | not many | few |
| not the same | different | not often | rarely |
| not allow | prevent | not stop | continue |
| not notice | overlook | not include | omit |

## Implicit negatives (watch; don't pile on *not*)
| class | words |
|---|---|
| Verbs | preclude, prevent, lack, fail, doubt, reject, avoid, deny, refuse, exclude, contradict, prohibit, bar |
| Prepositions | without, against, lacking, but for, except |
| Conjunctions | unless, except when |

## Rewrites
| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| Except when you have failed to submit applications without documentation, benefits will not be denied. | To receive benefits, submit your documents. |
| There should be no submission of payments without notification..., unless the payment does not exceed $100. | If you pay more than $100, notify this office first. |
| There is no reason not to believe that ... malfunctions ... cannot be anticipated. | Malfunctions ... will surprise us. |

Exception: keep a negative to emphasize the negative.
