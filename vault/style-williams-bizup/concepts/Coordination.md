# Coordination

**Definition:** Joining grammatically equivalent elements (words, phrases, clauses) with *and, but, or, nor* (or correlative pairs) so they stand as equals; "the foundation of a gracefully shaped sentence" (p. 156).

**Recognize it:** Lists and balanced pairs; Hoffman's *the minor, the unfinished, or even the botched*; *should—and should not—be done*.

**Rules:** parallel in grammar and in thought; order short to long; keep long coordinations traceable; correlatives for emphasis. Harder than modifiers but more graceful.

**Why it matters:** Replaces strings of tacked-on clauses with visible structure.
