# Thematic Thread

**Definition:** the string of related words (nouns, verbs, adjectives) that repeats a [[Theme]] through a passage. Counterpart of the [[Topic String]] (subject-level repetition).

**Two ways to build coherence (p. 91):** repeat character-concepts as topics (usually subjects); repeat other concepts as themes elsewhere in the sentences. Then stress the themes at the end of the introductory sentence.

**Use:** [[Thread themes through a passage]].
