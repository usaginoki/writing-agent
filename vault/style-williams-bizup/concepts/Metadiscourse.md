# Metadiscourse

**Definition:** Language that refers to the writer's intentions (*to sum up, candidly, I believe*), directions to the reader (*note that, consider now, as you see*), or the structure of the text (*first, second, therefore, however*); introduced in Lesson 4.
**Recognize:** Words talking about the text/the writer's act rather than the subject matter. Includes hedges and intensifiers.
**Why it matters:** Everything needs some, especially guiding words, but too much buries ideas; cut attribution and topic-announcing types; it can also fake a claim.
**Example:** "The last point I would like to make is that ... it is important to keep in mind that..." has 9 content words in 34.
