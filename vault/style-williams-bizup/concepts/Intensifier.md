# Intensifier

**Definition:** A word that increases the writer's stated certainty (*very, clearly, obviously, crucial, prove, of course*); a kind of [[Metadiscourse]]. The most common intensifier is the absence of a hedge.
**Recognize:** Adverbs, adjectives (*key, central, fundamental*), verbs (*show, prove, establish*), absolutes (*all, every, totally*). List: [[Patterns - Hedges and intensifiers]].
**Why it matters:** Readers reflexively doubt sentences starting *obviously, undoubtedly, it is clear that*; aggressive style provokes resistance, moderate style invites consideration.
**Example:** "censorship is totally a thing of the past" becomes "virtually a thing of the past."
