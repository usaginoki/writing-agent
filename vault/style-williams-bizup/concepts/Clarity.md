# Clarity

**Definition.** The quality of prose that lets readers grasp ideas without struggle; achieved by choices on the page that match how readers process sentences, not by grammatical precision. Belief 1: it is good; belief 2: anyone can learn it.

**Recognize it.** Clear: *We lost the election because the media did not support us.* Unclear: *Lack of media support was the cause of our election loss.*

**Why it matters.** Clear prose is not the same as correct prose: a sentence can be grammatically perfect and unreadable (L02's "information processing requirements" example). Clarity also helps the writer think. It is one aim; some occasions want [[Elegance]] too.

**Limits.** Clarity cannot rescue unorganized ideas ([[Coherence]], [[Motivation]]); it is judged from the reader's side.
