# Point

**Definition:** the claim a unit exists to make: for a document, the solution/main point/claim answering its problem; for a section or paragraph, what the unit adds up to (traditionally the topic sentence). Practical solution = an action to take; conceptual solution = something to understand/believe.

**Recognize it:** the sentence that, read alone, tells you what the unit concludes, containing the key terms the unit develops.

**Why it matters:** readers organize everything around it; "point" sentences form the logical outline of the document. If readers miss them they may judge writing incoherent.

**Where it goes:** the last sentence of the short opening segment (stress position), before the longer, more complex segment.

**Example:** "They did so successfully during instruction, but the effect was inconsistent and less than predicted, and six months later had no measurable effect."
