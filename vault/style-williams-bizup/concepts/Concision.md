# Concision

**Definition:** Using only enough words to say what you mean; style's "first grace" (compression, Marianne Moore). Achieved by deleting meaningless, repeated, implied, and negative words and replacing phrases with words.
**Recognize:** A sentence can lose words with no loss of meaning; readers can't find the content.
**Why it matters:** Clearing deadwood lets readers see a sentence's shape, but concision alone doesn't guarantee grace, and overdoing it gives terseness.
**Example:** "In my personal opinion, it is necessary that we should not ignore the opportunity to think over each and every suggestion offered." becomes "We should consider each suggestion."
