# Hedge

**Definition:** A word that qualifies the writer's certainty (*perhaps, may, seems, many, somewhat, suggest, indicate*); a kind of [[Metadiscourse]].
**Recognize:** Adverbs, adjectives (*most, some*), verbs (*may, tend, appear*). Full list: [[Patterns - Hedges and intensifiers]].
**Why it matters:** Signals balance of caution and confidence, shaping reader's judgment of your character. Too many sounds mealy-mouthed; too few is bulldozer assertion. Even Watson and Crick hedged.
**Example:** "This evidence suggests that aspects of Japanese and Western rhetoric differ..."
