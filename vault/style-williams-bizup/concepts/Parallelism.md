# Parallelism

**Definition:** Using the same grammatical form for coordinate elements (clause/clause, gerund/gerund, *that*-clause/*that*-clause).

**Recognize it:** Violations feel like "an offensive lack of parallelism": *recommends revising the curriculum and that the division be reorganized*. Parallelism can also be added to lengthen the second element of a short-to-long pair.

**Accepted exceptions:** noun phrase + *how* clause; adverb + prepositional phrase (p. 162). Correlative conjunctions must precede the same part of speech.

**Example:** "…recommends that the curriculum be revised… and that the division be reorganized…"
