# Passive and Active

**Definition:** *Active:* agent in subject, goal in direct object (`I lost the money`). *Passive:* `be` + past participle; goal is subject; agent in `by` phrase or deleted (`The money was lost [by me]`).
**Two senses:** grammatical vs impressionistic. A sentence can *feel* passive (flat, nominalized, no characters) while grammatically active (`Problem management requires cost control`).
**Why it matters:** "Use the active" has exceptions; choose by three questions: must readers know who; old-new flow; consistent point of view. Passives are also normal for procedural research verbs; actives for metadiscourse.
**Example:** `We must define the terms` (metadiscourse, active) vs `preparations were added` (procedure, passive).
