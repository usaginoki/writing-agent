# Stress

**Definition:** the most emphatic part of a sentence: its last few words, where the voice rises (heard even in silent reading).

**Recognize it:** read aloud; tap on the last 3-4 words ([[Stress test]]). Ending on low-meaning words (*according to most atmospheric scientists*; *it must be remembered*) makes a sentence end weakly.

**Why it matters:** stress sets emphasis and voice, just as [[Topic]] (the start) sets point of view. Put new, complex, or important material there ([[Old-New Information]]); put key themes there in opening sentences ([[Theme]]).
