# Coherence

**Definition:** the sense of what all the sentences add up to, as puzzle pieces add up to the picture on the box. Judged from how all sentences in a passage cumulatively *begin* (p. 67); in L06, also from themes and the stress of the opening sentence.

**Recognize it:** it is missing when (1) subjects are unrelated, (2) sentences share no common themes, (3) no sentence states what the passage is about.

**Why it matters:** readers call a writer competent only when the writing is coherent, not merely cohesive. Three supports: consistent topics ([[Topic String]]), threaded [[Theme]]s ([[Thematic Thread]]), and an opening sentence that stresses the themes ([[Announce key themes in the stress of the opening sentence]]).
