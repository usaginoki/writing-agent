# Issue

**Definition (working):** the short, easily grasped opening segment of any unit (sentence, paragraph, section, document) that frames the longer segment after it. It ends in the [[Point]] sentence and names the key themes that follow. Length is proportional: a sentence for a paragraph, a paragraph or more for a section, several paragraphs for a document, always much shorter than the rest.

**Recognize it:** at sentence level it is the subject/topic or main clause; at paragraph level one or two sentences; at document level the introduction (shared context + problem + solution).

**Why it matters:** readers judge a unit clear when it opens with a segment they can grasp and that frames the rest.

**Example:** "In this study, thirty sixth-grade students were taught to distinguish fact from opinion. They did so successfully..., but the effect was inconsistent and less than predicted..." (opening segment + point).
