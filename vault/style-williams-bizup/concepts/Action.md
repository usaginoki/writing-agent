# Action

**Definition:** What a character does, thinks or undergoes in the story level of a sentence (`walk, jump, argue, destabilize`). Distinct from the grammatical category *verb*.
**Recognize:** Ask "what happens?" Actions can sit in verbs (preferred), nominalizations (`jump`, `fright`, `intention`) or adjectives (`applicable`).
**Why it matters:** Clear sentences place important actions in verbs; the story level (variable order: character, action) should match the grammar level (fixed order: subject, verb).
**Example:** `the Wolf's jump... occurred` -> `the Wolf jumped`.
