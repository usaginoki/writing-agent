# Free Modifier

**Definition:** A modifying phrase that comments on the subject of the closest verb and can either end or begin the sentence ("free").

**Recognize it:** Usually begins with an *-ing* participle (*…, letting you extend the line*); also past participle (*…, driven by an insatiable curiosity and haunted by…*) or adjective (*…, aware that we faced another world war*). Unlike resumptive/summative modifiers it neither repeats a word nor sums up.

**Caution:** The implied subject must be the main subject, else it dangles ([[Give dangling modifiers an explicit subject]]).

**Example:** "Global warming will become a central political issue of the twenty-first century, raising questions whose answers will affect the standard of living in every Western nation." Moved: "Driven by an insatiable curiosity, Leonardo da Vinci was…"
