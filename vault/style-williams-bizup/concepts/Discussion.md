# Discussion

**Definition (working):** the longer, more complex segment following an [[Issue]]. It supports, develops, or explains the point and themes stated in the opening.

**Recognize it:** the body of a paragraph/section/document. Marks of a good one: consistent topics ([[Topic String]]), repeated key terms from the point sentence, old-before-new sentences ([[Old-New Information]]), every sentence relevant to the point, parts ordered chronologically/coordinately/logically.

**Why it matters:** readers use the themes announced at the end of the issue to organize what they read; if the discussion drops them, coherence fails.

**Example:** the sixth-grade study body repeats *instruction, inconsistently, predicted, no effect*, and ends by restating the point as a conclusion.
