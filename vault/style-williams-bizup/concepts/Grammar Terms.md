## Useful tests (compressed)
- **Whole subject:** put *who/what* before the verb and form a question; the fullest answer is the whole subject. Keep the simple subject near its verb.
- **Clause vs phrase:** can the verb be made past/present and agree with a subject? Yes: clause.
- **Adjective vs adverb:** *very* test; between *the* and noun.
- **Restrictive vs nonrestrictive:** see [[Set off nonrestrictive modifiers with commas]].
