# Relevance test

**Purpose:** find sentences readers will not see as relevant to the point.

## Procedure
1. State the unit's point.
2. For each sentence, classify: background/context; point; reason; evidence/data; explanation of reasoning or method; other views.
3. Flag sentences that fit none.

## Signals of a problem
Flagged sentences (spelling errors, essay length, organization in the fact/opinion study example).

## Fixes
[[Make every sentence relevant to the point]]
