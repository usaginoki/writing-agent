# Diagnose hidden characters

**Purpose:** Find the characters when the subject is not one (or when characters are missing). Applies when reading (to unravel) and writing (to revise).

## Procedure
1. Look at subjects first. If the main characters are not in a few short concrete words there, search elsewhere.
2. Check possessives, objects of prepositions (`by`, `of`), adjectives (`governmental` -> government; `theological` -> theologians), implied characters.
3. Find actions involving them, esp. nominalizations; list pairs (`market evolution -> markets evolve`; bracket implied: `[government] distorts`).
4. **Writing:** make each character the subject of its action verb; string pairs into sentences.
5. **Reading:** retell the story one character-action at a time; if that fails, list pairs and rewrite yourself.
6. If characters are totally absent, choose a doer ([[Name characters in subjects instead of deleting them]]).

## Signal of a problem
- Subject is an abstraction; `Who is doing what?` has no answer in the sentence.
