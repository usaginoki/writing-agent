# Diagnose characters and actions

**Purpose:** Explain why a sentence feels dense, and fix it even when it seems clear to you. Three steps: diagnose, analyze, rewrite (p. 35).

## Procedure
1. **Diagnose**
   - Skip a 4-5 word introductory phrase; underline the first 7-8 words ([[First seven words test]]).
   - Look for two results: (i) the simple subject is an abstract noun; (ii) 7-8 words pass before a verb appears.
2. **Analyze**
   - Decide the main characters, esp. flesh-and-blood ones; look in possessives, `by`/`of` objects, adjectives, or implied ([[Diagnose hidden characters]]).
   - Look for the actions they perform, esp. nominalizations; ask "Who is doing what?"
3. **Rewrite**
   - Nominalizations -> verbs; characters -> subjects of those verbs.
   - Rewrite with subordinators: *because, if, when, although, why, how, whether, that*.
   - With several clauses, decide how they fit: *X because Y; Since X, Y; If X, then Y; Although X, Y; X and/but/so Y*.

## Signal of a problem
- Abstract subject; late verb; vague verb (`was based`, `occurred`, `means`); many `of/by/in` phrases.

## Example
> ✗ The outsourcing of high-tech work to Asia by corporations means the loss of jobs for many American workers.
> ✓ Many middle-class American workers are losing their jobs, because corporations are outsourcing their high-tech work to Asia.
