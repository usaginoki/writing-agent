# Opening sentence test

**Purpose:** verify that a long paragraph (more than 5-6 sentences) has a framing sentence whose end names its themes.

## Procedure
1. Underline the sentence that best introduces/frames the paragraph. If you cannot do this quickly, the paragraph probably has a problem.
2. Circle the important words in it; they should sound like a title for the paragraph.
3. Check they are at the end (stress) and recur later as [[Theme]]s.

## Signals
- No identifiable framing sentence; key concept appears only late; circled words don't read like a title.
