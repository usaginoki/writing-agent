# Throat-clearing test

**Purpose:** find sentences with too much before the subject.

## Procedure
1. Mark everything before the subject/topic: transitions (*and, but, therefore*), attitude metadiscourse (*it is important to note*, *perhaps*, *for the most part*), time/place/manner phrases.
2. If several sentences in a row open this way, flag the passage.

## Signals
- Many words before the topic; reader can't tell what each sentence, or the passage, is about.
