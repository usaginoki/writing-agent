# Reader-swap test

**Purpose:** Check whether a text is ethical in the book's sense: would you accept receiving it?

## Procedure
1. Name the intended reader and what is at stake for them (money, safety, understanding).
2. Imagine being that reader with only the text in hand and the time they have.
3. Ask: do I learn who did/will do what, what it costs me, what I must do?
4. Ask: would the writer be content to receive this text in the same position?
5. Separate intent: careless (fix), innocent ignorance (fix), deliberate evasion (rewrite; consider refusing).

## Signals a problem
- The reader cannot tell who is responsible or how bad the outcome is.
- The writer protects self-interest by obscurity.
- The text satisfies a legal duty while evading the ethical one.
