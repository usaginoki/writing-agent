# Reader habit checklist for shape

**Purpose:** Have a reader identify your habitual shape problems (Exercise 10.4).

**Procedure:** Ask a reader to mark passages that were hard to get through and name the cause, using these questions:
- Too long to reach subjects and verbs?
- Subjects too long?
- Interruptions between subject-verb or verb-object?
- Too long to reach the point (sentence and passage)?
- Subordinate clause on subordinate clause on subordinate clause?
If the reader cannot name the cause, diagnose together; trust that *something* is amiss even if the reader can't say what.

**Fixes:** see [[L10 Shape]] principles.
