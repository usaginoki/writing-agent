# Stress test

**Purpose:** check that sentence endings carry the words that deserve emphasis.

## Procedure
1. Read the sentence aloud.
2. At the last 3-4 words, raise your voice and tap a finger hard on the table.
3. Does it feel right? If you tap on words that don't deserve emphasis (metadiscourse, repeated words, redundant tails), find words that do and move them nearer the end.
4. Over a page (Ex. 6.3): count wrong vs. right stresses, note patterns.

## Signals
- Weak ending, repeated word, attribution or hedge in last position.

## Fix with
[[Put the words you want emphasized in stress position]] · [[Trim the end of the sentence]] · [[Shift peripheral ideas to the left]] · [[Shift new information to the right]] · [[Avoid ending a sentence on a repeated word]]
