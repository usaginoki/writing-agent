# Active passive choice test

**Purpose:** Decide whether a verb should be active or passive; also decide whether a metadiscourse verb should be first-person active.

## Procedure
1. Identify grammatical voice: passive = `be` + past participle; goal in subject; agent in `by` or dropped. Separate this from the *feel* of passivity (nominalized abstract flat).
2. Ask three questions: Must readers know who? Which voice gives smoother old-to-new flow? Which gives consistent point of view?
3. Is it a metadiscourse verb (writer's own thinking/writing)? -> active `I/we`. Research procedure anyone could do? -> usually passive.
4. List the subjects of consecutive sentences for unmotivated shifts.

## Signal of a problem
- Passive that hides a needed agent; long new subject followed by familiar object; jumping subjects; `It is argued that` for the writer's own claim; first-person narrative of routine procedure.
