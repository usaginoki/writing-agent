# Point-first test

**Purpose:** Check that a long sentence (or passage) states its point before its support.

**Procedure:**
1. Find the key claim ([[Point]]).
2. Locate it: beginning, middle, or end? Count the words before it.
3. Check whether readers can see the relevance of the opening words only after reaching the end (the sentence "feels backward").
4. Repeat for paragraphs, sections, and the whole document.

**Signal:** Point in the middle or end, or dribbled out = revise.

**Fix:** [[Open a long sentence with its point]].
