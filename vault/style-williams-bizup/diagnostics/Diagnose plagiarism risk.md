# Diagnose plagiarism risk

**Purpose:** Catch passages in which an informed reader could think you take credit for others' words or ideas.

## Procedure
1. For every claim, fact, method or phrase not yours, locate the note/source.
2. Exact words: quotation marks or block? Citation with page?
3. Paraphrase: cited? Compare with source: same sentence structure, only synonyms swapped?
4. Distinctive phrases of the source reused without quotes?
5. Idea or method borrowed from a source without citation?
6. Online material: cited like any other?

## What signals a problem
- Any "no" above -> [[Cite every word and idea you borrow]], [[Paraphrase in a new structure, not with synonyms]], [[Quote striking words once]].
- Notes that don't mark quotation vs paraphrase -> [[Take notes that keep source words visibly separate]].
