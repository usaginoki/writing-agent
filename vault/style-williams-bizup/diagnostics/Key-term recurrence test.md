# Key-term recurrence test

**Purpose:** check that the key terms in the point sentence recur in the body (and that nothing important in the body is missing from the point).

## Procedure
1. Bold the key terms in the point sentence.
2. Scan the body: mark each recurrence (exact wording).
3. In reverse, list the recurrent terms in the body and check they appear in the point sentence.

## Signals of a problem
Terms in the opening never return; recurring terms in the body never named in the opening; synonyms substituted for key terms.

## Fixes
[[Name key themes toward the end of the point sentence]] · [[Repeat key themes in the body]]
