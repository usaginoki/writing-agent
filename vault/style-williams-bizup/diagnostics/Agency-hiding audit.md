# Agency-hiding audit

**Purpose:** Detect passages where subject choices conceal who acts, who pays, or who is at risk.

## Procedure
1. Boldface the subject of every clause.
2. Tag each: person/group (name), organization, reader, or object/abstraction.
3. Tag verbs: active action, passive, intransitive (*occur*), nominalized.
4. List the real actors and affected parties; check each appears as a subject at least where their responsibility or risk is the point.
5. For each hiding spot, ask: neutral habit, kindness, or evasion?
6. Compare with a rewritten version with *we* and *you* as subjects.

## Signals a problem
- Company never a subject; reader only possessive.
- Harmful actions appear as nominalizations or agentless passives.
- Grammar implies coercion (*necessity constrains*) that you have not argued for.
