# Diagnose hedge and intensifier balance

**Purpose:** Check whether the writer's stated certainty fits the evidence and reader expectations (the "Goldilocks rule": not too much, not too little, just right; develop and trust your ear).

## Procedure
1. Mark hedges and intensifiers in the claim ([[Patterns - Hedges and intensifiers]]). Mark claims with no hedge: that is an intensifier.
2. Too many hedges stacked (*seems, some, suggest, certain, could, possibly*) = too uncertain.
3. Absolutes or *prove / obviously* with no hedge = too certain.
4. Try the mid-point: one hedge (*suggests, indicates, many, most, virtually*).
5. Reflex check: a sentence opening *obviously / undoubtedly / it is clear that / there is no question* makes most readers think the opposite.
6. Ask a reader whether the tone is mealy or bulldozer.

Fixes: [[Calibrate hedges]], [[Calibrate intensifiers]]. See [[Hedge]], [[Intensifier]].
