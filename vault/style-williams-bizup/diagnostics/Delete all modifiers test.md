# Delete all modifiers test

**Purpose:** Separate needed modifiers from reflexive ones.

## Procedure
1. Delete every adverb and every adjective before a noun in the passage.
2. Read the stripped passage.
3. Restore *only* modifiers readers need to understand it.

## Signal
- Passage reads fine without most of them: they were padding (Tannen's passage lost *absolute, irreconcilable, continually, daily*).
- Meaning collapses or becomes vague: restore that modifier.
