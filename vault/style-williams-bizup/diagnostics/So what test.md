# So what test

**Purpose:** find or verify the cost of a problem.

## Procedure
1. Underline the condition.
2. Imagine a skeptical reader asking "So what?" right after it.
3. Read the next sentences: do they answer?
4. Practical: answer must be palpable unhappiness (injury, money, loss). Conceptual: answer must be a larger, more important unknown.
5. Test with other readers' eyes: would a different reader (publicist, callous reader) accept the cost?

## Signals of a problem
No answer; answer only restates the condition; cost matters to you but not to your readers.

## Fixes
[[Find the cost by asking So what]] · [[State practical costs that matter to your readers]] · [[Frame conceptual problems as ignorance plus a larger question]]
