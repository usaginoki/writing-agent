# Preview a difficult document by its points

**Purpose:** a reading technique (and a way to check your own draft's outline): extract the structure before reading in detail.

## Procedure
1. Highlight the question in the problem statement and the main claim answering it (look in the conclusion if absent; if still absent, ask "What question does this answer?").
2. For each section highlight introduction, point, key concepts (at the section's end if not at its start).
3. Read just the highlighted parts.
4. Then read in detail.

## Signals of a problem
For your own draft: if you can't highlight these, readers can't either.
