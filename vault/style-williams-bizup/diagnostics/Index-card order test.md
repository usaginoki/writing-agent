# Index-card order test

**Purpose:** check whether the order of a document/section is one readers can see, and detect default chronological structure.

## Procedure
1. Highlight each paragraph's point; copy onto index cards.
2. Shuffle; give to a reader to order.
3. Compare with your order; note what must change.
4. Also name the ordering principle (chronological, coordinate, logical) and check for signal words.
5. Check whether the draft is just a narrative of your thinking.

## Signals of a problem
Reader's order differs; chronological structure by default; no nameable principle.

## Fixes
[[Order parts chronologically, coordinately, or logically and signal it]] · [[Do not narrate your thinking]]
