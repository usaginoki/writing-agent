# Relative pronoun trace

**Purpose:** Untangle or detect chains of relative clauses (Quick Tip, p. 152).

**Procedure:**
1. Mark each *who, which, that* after the main clause.
2. For each, identify its antecedent (what it refers to).
3. Reread substituting nouns for the pronouns.

**Signal:** 3+ stacked relatives, or a *which* referring to a whole clause = [[Sprawl]].

**Fix:** [[Do not chain subordinate clauses after the main clause]]; [[Patterns - Revising sprawl]].
