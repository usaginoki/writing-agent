# Sprawl read-aloud test

**Purpose:** Find shapeless long sentences that are invisible to the writer ("intractable subjectivity").

**Procedure:**
1. Pick out sentences longer than two lines.
2. Read each aloud (or have someone read it to you).
3. Note: running out of breath before a place to integrate the parts; stopping and starting because of interruptions; hesitation or stumbling.
4. Name the cause: late verb, trailing subordinate clauses, interruptions.

**Signal:** Any of those = readers will struggle too (p. 144, 152).

**Fixes:** [[Get to the subject of the main clause quickly]], [[Do not interrupt subject and verb]], [[Do not interrupt verb and object]], [[Do not chain subordinate clauses after the main clause]]. See [[Sprawl]].
