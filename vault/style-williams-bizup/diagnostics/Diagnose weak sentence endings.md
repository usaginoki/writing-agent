# Diagnose weak sentence endings

**Purpose:** Find important sentences that trail off instead of ending on stress.

## Procedure
1. For each key sentence, mark the last 3-4 words.
2. Classify the final word: preposition/pronoun/adverb (weak); adjective (medium); noun (strong); nominalization or coordinate noun pair (strongest).
3. Check whether the new, important information is at the end ([[Old-New Information]]).
4. Check for an *of* phrase ending, an echo of an earlier term, or a longest-last series.

## Signals a problem
- Sentence ends on *to*, *with*, *of it*, or a vague adverb.
- Final words repeat old information.
- Ending is a flat string of modifiers.
