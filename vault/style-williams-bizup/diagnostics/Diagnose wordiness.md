# Diagnose wordiness

**Purpose:** Find what to cut in a sentence; the book says you must "inch your way through every sentence."

## Procedure (one pass per question, in order)
1. Meaningless words? Tics (kind of, actually, really...) -> [[Delete meaningless words]].
2. Doubled words? "X and Y" synonyms -> [[Delete redundant pairs]].
3. Words implied by others? Modifiers, category nouns, whole clauses -> [[Delete redundant modifiers]], [[Delete redundant categories]], [[Delete obvious implications]].
4. Could a word replace a phrase? Stock phrases, nominalizations -> [[Replace a phrase with a word]].
5. Negatives? explicit or implicit -> [[Change negatives to affirmatives]].
6. Adjectives/adverbs? Strike all, restore needed -> [[Delete useless adjectives and adverbs]]; see [[Delete all modifiers test]].
7. Metadiscourse? -> [[Metadiscourse ratio test]].
8. Hedges/intensifiers? -> [[Diagnose hedge and intensifier balance]].

## Signal
- Fewer words convey the same content after deletion; or the sentence content is about nine words in thirty-four.
- After cutting, check not terse: [[Terseness test]].
