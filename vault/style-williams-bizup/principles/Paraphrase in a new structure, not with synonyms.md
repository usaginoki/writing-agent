# Paraphrase in a new structure, not with synonyms

**Rule:** A paraphrase must be recast in a new sentence structure; swapping synonyms into the source's structure is plagiarism even with a citation. Always cite a paraphrase.
**Why (reader effect):** Readers see your own thinking; closely tracked sentences show you borrowing the structure.

## Diagnose
- Compare your sentence with the source side by side. Same order, same clause structure, a few synonyms? Too close.

## Revise
1. Set the source aside; state the point you want to make in your own words, with citation (Lipson's technique, p. 235).
2. Compare; if several identical words or only synonym swaps, rewrite.
3. Prefer a signal phrase naming the author.

## Examples
> Original: The drama is the most social of literary forms, since it stands in so direct a relationship to its audience.
> ✗ The theater is a very social genre because it relates so directly with its viewers.
> ✓ Levin claims that we experience the theater as the most social form of literature because we see it taking place before us.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- None stated in source.
