# Observe real and social rules

**Rule:** Follow Real Rules (articles before nouns; verbs agree with subjects; pronouns agree with referents) and Social Rules (Standard vs. nonstandard forms). Do not follow them self-consciously; just do them and fix slips.
**Why (reader effect):** Violations mark the writer as unschooled; at best readers notice and judge, at worst they stigmatize. Observing them goes unnoticed.

## Diagnose
- Subject-verb or pronoun-referent mismatch; nonstandard grammar (double negatives like *don't have no*, *ain't*, *irregardless*) in text meant for serious readers.

## Revise
1. Correct to Standard English where the audience is serious/educated.
2. Do not apply rule-lawyer logic ("ain't is logical"): social convention outweighs it.

## Examples
> ✗ He don't have no money.
> ✓ He doesn't have any money.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Dialogue or deliberate register choices. Note: grammar errors do not indicate low intelligence or morals; do not use them to look down on writers.
