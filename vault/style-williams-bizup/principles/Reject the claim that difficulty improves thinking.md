# Reject the claim that difficulty improves thinking

**Rule:** Do not defend obscure writing as good for readers; no evidence supports that harder reading produces deeper understanding, and substantial evidence contradicts it.
**Why (reader effect):** Added difficulty consumes the effort readers need for the ideas themselves.

## Diagnose
- Are you justifying complexity as "making readers think"? Replace with an explicit task if you want effortful engagement.

## Revise
1. Remove gratuitous difficulty.
2. If you want readers to think hard, pose a hard question, not a hard sentence.

## Examples
> ✗ "Hard prose is productive struggle."
> ✓ Clear sentences, a hard problem.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- None stated in source beyond genuinely difficult subject matter.
