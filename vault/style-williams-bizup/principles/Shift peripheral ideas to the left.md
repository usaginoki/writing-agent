# Shift peripheral ideas to the left

**Rule:** Move qualifiers, attributions, and metadiscourse from the end to the front (or inside) so the main point sits in stress.
**Why (reader effect):** anticlimactic endings (*for the most part*, *it must be remembered*) drain emphasis.

## Diagnose
- Does the sentence end in a hedge, attribution, or "it must be remembered"-type phrase?

## Revise
1. Move that element to the start.
2. Check the last words now carry the point.

## Examples
> ✗ The data offered to prove ESP are weak, for the most part.
> ✓ For the most part, the data offered to prove ESP are weak.

> ✗ Job opportunities in computer programming are getting scarcer, it must be remembered.
> ✓ It must be remembered that job opportunities in computer programming are getting scarcer.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Particularly avoid anticlimactic metadiscourse at the end; otherwise none stated. Don't leave it at the front if it creates [[Clear throat-clearing before the subject]].
