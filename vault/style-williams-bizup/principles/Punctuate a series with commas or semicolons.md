# Punctuate a series with commas or semicolons

**Rule:** Three or more items: commas between them if none has internal punctuation; semicolons if any does. Final comma before *and* (serial comma): choice (most insist on it); be consistent. Same for a series of short sentences (commas) vs punctuated ones (semicolons).
**Why (reader effect):** Semicolons show how readers should group items.

## Diagnose
- Any commas inside an item? Switch to semicolons.
- Is the serial comma used consistently throughout?

## Revise
1. Decide on the serial comma; apply everywhere.
2. Use semicolons for complex items (the final one before *and*).

## Examples
> ✓ His wit, his charm, and his loyalty made him our friend. (also correct without comma before *and*)
> ✓ ...economical, organic, and logical; fascinating, yet not exotic; clear, but complicated enough...

## When not to apply / exceptions
- None stated in source (both comma styles correct).
