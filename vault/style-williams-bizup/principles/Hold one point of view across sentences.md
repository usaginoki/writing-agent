# Hold one point of view across sentences

**Rule:** Pick one character as the viewpoint and keep it as a consistent sequence of subjects; use passives where needed to keep that character in the subject slot. Do not switch characters for no reason.
**Why (reader effect):** Unmotivated shifts of subject make a passage jumpy and the story incoherent.

## Diagnose
- List the subjects of consecutive sentences; do they alternate between two parties without purpose?

## Revise
1. Decide whose story it is.
2. Make that party subject: active verbs for the Allies, passives for Germany (or vice versa).
3. Keep subjects a coherent sequence ([[Thematic Thread]]).

## Examples
> ✓ (Allied view) By early 1945, the Allies had essentially defeated Germany... they were bombing it around the clock.
> ✓ (German view) By early 1945, Germany had essentially been defeated... Its borders had been breached, and it was being bombed around the clock.
> ✗ The Allies had defeated Germany. Its borders had been breached, and they were bombing it... Germany was not so devastated, however, that the Allies... (shifting)

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Shift deliberately when the story genuinely changes focus.
