# Delete useless adjectives and adverbs

**Rule:** Delete every adverb and every adjective before a noun, then restore only those readers need to understand the passage.
**Why (reader effect):** Reflexive modifiers pad prose and blur the claim.

## Diagnose
- Strike all adverbs and pre-noun adjectives in a passage.
- Read the stripped version; restore only a modifier whose absence changes or obscures meaning.
- Tannen passage: struck *absolute and irreconcilable, continually, static and limiting, Chinese, irreconcilable polar, as much as possible, abstract, daily*; kept modifiers like *principles at war* that do work.

## Revise
1. Delete all.
2. Restore the necessary few.
3. Compare with [[Delete redundant modifiers]] (modifiers implied by their head word).

## Examples
> ✗ ...translate this abstract idea into daily practice?
> ✓ ...translate this idea into practice? (Tannen's text with modifiers struck; restore any you judge needed.)

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Modifiers that restrict, distinguish or define terms are needed. The book's test is reader need, not a ban.
