# Use one standard citation style

**Rule:** Cite fully, accurately and in the style your reader expects: Chicago (humanities, some social sciences), MLA (literary studies), APA (social sciences). Find a guide.
**Why (reader effect):** Readers who see sloppy small details doubt you on big ones; the chain of thinking must be a chain of trust (p. 238).

## Diagnose
- Which style does the audience/instructor require? Is it applied consistently (punctuation, order, dates)?

## Revise
1. Pick the required style; follow a guide.
2. Check every citation against the notes.
