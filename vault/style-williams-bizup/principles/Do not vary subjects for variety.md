# Do not vary subjects for variety

**Rule:** Ignore the advice "vary how you begin sentences" when it makes you change topics. Repeat topics; vary wording only in extreme cases.
**Why (reader effect):** you see the repetition because you wrote it; readers focus on ideas and rarely notice, but they do notice random topic shifts.

## Diagnose
- Same topic repeated in same words and same position across many sentences ("moral climate ... moral climate ... moral climate")?

## Revise
1. Keep the topic; vary a few words that refer to it (*This climate*, *morality*).
2. Be cautious: most writers change topics too often.

## Examples
> ✗ "Moral climate" is created... Moral climate results... moral climate acts... A moral climate encourages...
> ✓ "Moral climate" is created... This climate results... In this light, morality acts... A moral climate encourages...

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Only when identical words repeat in identical positions to excess.
