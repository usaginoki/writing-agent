# Echo an earlier word at the sentence end

**Rule:** Let the stressed final word or phrase repeat or answer the sound or meaning of an earlier one (echoing salience).
**Why (reader effect):** The echo gives the ending special emphasis and a sense of closure; the sentence seems to curl back on itself.

## Diagnose
- Find a sentence whose final point restates its opening term. Is the restatement in the stress position, and does it echo in sound (manner/matter) or word root (style/stylish)?

## Revise
1. Choose the key term of the sentence's opening.
2. Reintroduce it (or a sound/meaning relative) at the stress position.
3. Pair verbs by voice reversal: *shapes* / *is shaped by*.

## Examples
> ...style the centaur; ...the style is the man. (Gay)
> ...manner is indissolubly linked to matter; style shapes, and in turn is shaped by, substance.
> It seems frivolous, almost inappropriate, to be stylish about style.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Overuse reads as cute; it works at the close of a key claim.
