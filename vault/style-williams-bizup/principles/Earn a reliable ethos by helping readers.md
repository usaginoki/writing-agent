# Earn a reliable ethos by helping readers

**Rule:** Go the extra step to help readers understand; the character readers infer (accessible or difficult, trustworthy or deceitful, candid or aloof) hardens into your reputation.
**Why (reader effect):** We trust writers with a reputation as thoughtful and considerate; carelessness costs more than attention.

## Diagnose
- What would a reader infer about your character from this text: evasive, lazy, self-protective?
- Do readers have to guess at the main point or the responsible party?

## Revise
1. Remove sources of strain (see Lessons 3-10).
2. Be candid about who, what, how much.

## Examples
> ✗ Rate notice that never names the company.
> ✓ We can now charge you more.
