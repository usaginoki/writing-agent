# Clear throat-clearing before the subject

**Rule:** Don't pile words between the sentence start and its subject/topic: transitions, attitude markers, time/place/manner phrases.
**Why (reader effect):** readers want to reach a subject quickly; repeated delay hides what each sentence, and the whole passage, is about.

## Diagnose
- Count words before the subject. Stack check: transition (*and therefore*) + attitude metadiscourse (*it is important to note*) + time/place (*in Eastern states since 1980*) then subject ([[Throat-clearing test]]).

## Revise
1. Cut or shift [[Metadiscourse]] (attitude markers, "it is important to note").
2. Keep one transition at most; trim or move time/place phrases after the subject.
3. Get to the topic.

## Examples
> ✗ And, therefore, it is important to note that, in Eastern states since 1980, acid rain has become a serious problem.
> ✓ Since 1980, therefore, acid rain has become a serious problem in the Eastern states.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- A few openers are fine; the harm is opening several sentences this way.
