# Use chiasmus for special effect

**Rule:** For a special effect, reverse the order of elements in the second half of a balanced sentence (1A1B : 2B2A) so the second half mirrors the first.
**Why (reader effect):** Mirror structure gives a crossing, finished quality; the ending feels inevitable. The book calls it of interest mainly to those fascinated by arcane figures.

## Diagnose
- Look for a sentence with two parallel halves in the same order (1A1B : 2A2B). Would a reversed second half sharpen the contrast or close the thought?

## Revise
1. Label the elements of the first half (A, B, C...).
2. Rewrite the second half in reverse order.
3. Check meaning and sound pairs still align and that it does not read as forced.

## Examples
> ✗ A concise style can improve both our own thinking and our readers' understanding.
> ✓ A concise style can improve not only our own thinking but the understanding of our readers.
> Turner's closer is a quadruple chiasmus: the temporary sacrifice of his individual freedom and his life, lest that freedom be lost forever.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Turner's sentence "may be over the top"; extravagant constructions are not models for everyday prose.
- Rare use only.
