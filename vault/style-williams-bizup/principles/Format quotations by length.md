# Format quotations by length

**Rule:** Four lines or fewer: run in with quotation marks. Five lines or more: block quotation (indent like a paragraph, no quotation marks), introduced by words ending in a period or colon, or completing the grammar of your introductory sentence (then punctuate as if run in). Never begin a quotation in running text and complete it in a block. A handful of words: quote only if striking or important; ordinary phrases need nothing.
**Why (reader effect):** Block form signals a long extract; fragments quoted selectively keep your voice in control.

## Diagnose
- Count lines. Does the quote start inline and finish as a block?
- Quoted words are ordinary? Remove quotes. Striking? Add quotes and citation.

## Revise
1. Choose run-in vs block by length.
2. Quote only the distinctive words ([[Quote striking words once]]).
3. Introduce blocks with a lead-in.

## Examples
> ✓ ...because technology "begets more technology." It is, as Diamond puts it, an "autocatalytic process."

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Prefer shortening long quotations to key phrases woven in.
