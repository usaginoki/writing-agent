# Name key themes toward the end of the point sentence

**Rule:** In the point sentence (toward its end), use the specific concepts you will develop as distinctive themes of the unit. For the document: its main themes; for each section: that section's themes.
**Why (reader effect):** readers use those terms to organize their understanding; unannounced key terms (*inconsistently, no measurable effect*) first surfacing deep in the body leave readers without a frame.

## Diagnose
- List the recurring terms in the body. Are they in the opening sentence?
- Does the opening instead use general words that the body never repeats?

## Revise
1. Pick the 2-4 terms the body develops.
2. Rewrite the point sentence so they appear, ideally late in it (stress).
3. Keep the same wording later ([[Repeat key themes in the body]]).

## Examples
> ✗ "...to determine the effectiveness of eight weeks of training..." (themes absent)
> ✓ "...the effect was **inconsistent** and **less than predicted**, and six months after... **no measurable effect**."
