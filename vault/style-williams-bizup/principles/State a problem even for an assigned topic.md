# State a problem even for an assigned topic

**Rule:** An assigned topic does not excuse you from a problem statement. If the assignment says *discuss, explain, analyze*, find the question behind it; if it states a question without significance, find the answer to "So what?".
**Why (reader effect):** papers with complete problem statements are better written and better received.

## Diagnose
- Complete: "I am writing about..." / "I care because..." / "The point I make is..." (Exercise 7.2). Can't finish the second? Rethink the topic.
- Can you describe a real person who would care and say why (letter exercise)?

## Revise
1. Write topic, personal reason, main claim, one imagined reader, and a letter to that reader on why she should care.
2. Reuse phrases from the letter in the problem statement.

## Examples
> ✗ assignment: "Analyze the space shuttle Columbia accident."
> ✓ ask what question lies behind it and what larger matter it bears on.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- None stated in source.
