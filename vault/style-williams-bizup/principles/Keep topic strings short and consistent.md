# Keep topic strings short and consistent

**Rule:** Across a passage, keep subjects short and drawn from a small set of related ideas, usually the main characters.
**Why (reader effect):** readers are confused when each sentence opens with a different subject; consistent topics tell them what the passage is globally about ([[Coherence]]).

## Diagnose
- Run the [[Topic string test]]. Many unrelated subjects, or abstract/long ones, signal trouble.
- Quick Tip: before drafting a section list its characters (people and concepts); try to make them the subjects of most sentences; if one is absent for several sentences, you may be off track.

## Revise
1. Name the topics the passage is "about".
2. Rewrite subjects to use them ([[Make subjects name topics]]).
3. Check familiarity.

## Examples
> ✗ Competition by Asian companies with American companies in the Pacific is the first phase of this study. Labor costs ... are examined. A plan that will show American industry how to restructure its facilities will be developed...
> ✓ In the first phase of this study, we examine how Asian companies compete... We examine in particular their labor costs... We develop from this study a plan...

> Topic string after revision: *readers, they, they, they, topics, readers, that, they*.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Don't vary subjects for variety ([[Do not vary subjects for variety]]); but if exactly the same words appear in the same positions, vary a few ([[Do not vary subjects for variety]]).
