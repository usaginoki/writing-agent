# Accept formulaic structure for clarity

**Rule:** Use the framing patterns even though they may feel cut and dried to you; most readers read to understand, not for aesthetic pleasure, and will be grateful.
**Why (reader effect):** readers with little time focus on substance rather than critiquing form; the writer alone is conscious of the formula.

## Diagnose
- Are you avoiding a clear structure out of fear of boredom/lost creativity? Who is the reader and how patient?

## Revise
1. Apply the patterns by default.
2. Depart only for exploratory literary essays for patient readers.

## Examples
> n/a (stance principle). Contrast: a point-first, theme-naming opening vs. a rambling one.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Literary essays exploring thoughts as you have them, for readers with time and patience: "go to it."
