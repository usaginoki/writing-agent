# Avoid ending a sentence on a repeated word

**Rule:** If the final word repeats one used a few words earlier, use a pronoun, rewrite, or delete the repeat.
**Why (reader effect):** the voice drops on a repeated word, so the sentence ends flat and the stress lands wrongly.

## Diagnose
- Read aloud; the last words repeat something just said. Tap test feels wrong ([[Stress test]]).

## Revise
1. Replace the repeated noun with a pronoun (stress moves to the word before it).
2. Or delete the repeat (ellipsis).

## Examples
> ✗ ... when you repeat that word, your voice drops. Instead of repeating the noun, use a pronoun.
> ✗ It is sometimes possible to represent a complex idea in a simple sentence, but more often you cannot represent it in that kind of sentence.
> ✓ It is sometimes possible to represent a complex idea in a simple sentence, but more often you cannot.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- The book calls this "a fine point". Rhetorical figures that end sentences are in Lesson 11.
