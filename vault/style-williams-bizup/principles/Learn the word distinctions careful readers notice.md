# Learn the word distinctions careful readers notice

**Rule:** Use the traditional senses of commonly confused words (see [[Patterns - Word distinctions]]), always avoid *irregardless*, and keep Latin/Greek plurals plural (*data, criteria, media, phenomena*). Most readers do not care, but the few who do carry weight.
**Why (reader effect):** Cheap insurance and a signal of precision; erroneous use costs credibility with exactly the readers whose judgment matters.

## Diagnose
- Scan for the listed words and for homophone pairs (accept/except, affect/effect, principal/principle).
- Singular use of plurals like *data* as *a datum*.

## Revise
1. Replace the misused word with the intended one, or rewrite with a plain word.
2. If unsure, choose a plain synonym.

## Examples
> ✗ The alphabet is comprised by its letters.
> ✓ The alphabet comprises its letters. / Letters constitute the alphabet.
> ✓ We the People ..., in order to form a more perfect union (absolute words with *more*: generally worth avoiding, but not a bar).

## When not to apply / exceptions
- *like* for *as*, *hopefully*, *finalize*, verb *impact* are hobgoblins, not required distinctions (do not flag).
