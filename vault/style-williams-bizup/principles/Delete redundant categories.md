# Delete redundant categories

**Rule:** Cut the general noun that names the category a specific word already implies (*pink in color*, *period of time*, *membrane area*).
**Why (reader effect):** Every word implies its category; naming it adds length only.

## Diagnose
- Look for general nouns: size, shape, nature, type, area, color, time, state, manner, system, activities, appearance, character, condition, field.
- Frame: "[specific word] + in/of + [category]".

## Revise
1. Delete the category word and its preposition.
2. If it leaves an adjective hanging, convert: adjective + *manner* becomes an adverb (*accurate manner* to *accurately*).
3. Or convert adjective + *system/activities* into a noun (*educational system* to *education*; *recreational activities* to *recreation*).

## Examples
> ✗ During that period of time, the membrane area became pink in color and shiny in appearance.
> ✓ During that period, the membrane became pink and shiny.
> ✗ The holes must be aligned in an accurate manner. / ✓ ...aligned accurately.
> ✗ In the area of education, tight financial conditions are forcing school boards to cut nonessential expenses.
> ✓ Tight finances are forcing school boards to cut nonessentials.
