# Spend revision effort on beginnings

**Rule:** Budget drafting and revision time mostly for beginnings: the introduction to the whole, then introductions to major sections, then to subsections and long paragraphs, then sentence beginnings.
**Why (reader effect):** with beginnings right, the rest tends to take care of itself.

## Diagnose
- Compare time spent on openings versus middles.

## Revise
1. Order of work: document intro > section intros > subsection/long paragraph openings > sentence beginnings.

## Examples
> ✓ the Alzheimer's model: each unit opens with a framing segment ending in a point and themes.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- None stated in source.
