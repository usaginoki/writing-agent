# Quote striking words once

**Rule:** If you repeat a few words from a source and they are strikingly original or especially important, put them in quotation marks and cite the first time. After that, you may reuse them without quotes or citation.
**Why (reader effect):** Reusing distinctive phrasing unmarked looks like claiming credit for it.

## Diagnose
- List phrases you borrowed from the source; which are distinctive (metaphor, coinage, term of art)?

## Revise
1. Quote and cite on first use.
2. Later uses: plain.
3. Ordinary phrases (e.g., "the importance of the original invention") need neither.

## Examples
> ✓ ...technology "begets more technology." ... later: As one invention begets another one and that one still another, the process becomes self-sustaining...

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Words anyone might use: treat as your own.
