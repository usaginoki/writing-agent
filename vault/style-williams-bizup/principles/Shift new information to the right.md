# Shift new information to the right

**Rule:** Move new material to the end of the sentence; put the element that links back to context ahead of it.
**Why (reader effect):** the most common way to manage stress; new info lands in stress, old stays in topic.

## Diagnose
- Is the new element in the subject while a linking phrase (*more difficult than ...*) trails?

## Revise
1. Swap order so the link-back element opens and the new element ends.
2. Use inversion, passive, or *there/what/it* shifts as needed ([[Use there, what, and it shifts to control stress]]).

## Examples
> ✗ Questions about the ethics of withdrawing intravenous feeding are more difficult.
> ✓ More difficult are questions about the ethics of withdrawing intravenous feeding.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- None stated in source.
