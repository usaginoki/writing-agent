# Use the principles to mentally revise difficult reading

**Rule:** When prose is hard to read, find on the page whether the difficulty is necessary (complex material) or gratuitous (complex writing); if gratuitous, rewrite it mentally into a clear version.
**Why (reader effect):** Prevents blaming yourself for others' bad writing and prevents imitating it.

## Diagnose
- Is difficulty tied to the subject, or to abstract, indirect wording (nominalizations, buried actions)?

## Revise
1. Locate the abstract/indirect features.
2. Restate in plain terms (who does what).
3. Do not copy the original's style in your own writing.

## Examples
> ✗ Recognition of the fact that systems differ ... can serve as the basis for serious consideration of the problems ...
> ✓ When we recognize that languages have different grammars, we can consider the problems of those who translate ...
