# Work topic phrases into the subject

**Rule:** Replace "In regard to X," / "So far as X is concerned," with X as the sentence's subject.
**Why (reader effect):** These constructions flag a topic already mentioned; making it the subject (see [[Topic]]) is shorter and clearer.

## Diagnose
- Look for sentence openers *in regard to, as for, so far as ... is concerned, concerning, with respect to* followed by a clause about the same thing.

## Revise
1. Move the topic noun phrase into subject position.
2. Rework the rest of the clause so the verb follows directly.
3. Delete the opener.

## Examples
> ✗ In regard to a vigorous style, the most important feature is a short, concrete subject followed by a forceful verb.
> ✓ The most important feature of a vigorous style is a short, concrete subject followed by a forceful verb.
> ✗ So far as China's industrial development is concerned, it has long surpassed that of Japan.
> ✓ China has long surpassed Japan's industrial development.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- "Usually" only; the construction can mark a deliberate topic shift. None otherwise stated in source.
