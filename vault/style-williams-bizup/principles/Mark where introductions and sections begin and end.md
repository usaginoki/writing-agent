# Mark where introductions and sections begin and end

**Rule:** Make readers able to locate where the introduction ends and the body begins, and where each section ends and the next begins. Open each section with a heading containing its key themes; drop headings in the final draft only if your field doesn't use them.
**Why (reader effect):** readers who can't find the boundaries may miss the problem and solution.

## Diagnose
- Can you draw a line after the introduction quickly? If not, neither can readers.
- Does each heading carry the section's themes rather than a generic label?

## Revise
1. Insert or clarify headings with key terms.
2. Make the opening segment of each section distinct from its body.

## Examples
> ✗ heading: "Results" (no themes)
> ✓ heading naming the section's themes (the book states the principle; no full example).

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Fields that don't use headings: delete them for the final draft but keep the structure.
