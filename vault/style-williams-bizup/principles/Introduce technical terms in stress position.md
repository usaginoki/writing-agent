# Introduce technical terms in stress position

**Rule:** Put unfamiliar technical terms at the end of their sentences, with familiar material before them; state implicit information explicitly. Reuse the term as old information in the next sentence's opening.
**Why (reader effect):** terms stacked at sentence beginnings force readers to process new vocabulary before they have context; at the end, each is introduced with a lead-in and then becomes old.

## Diagnose
- Highlight technical terms. Are they at the starts of sentences, with familiar words at the ends? Does the text assume definitions?

## Revise
1. Rewrite so each sentence's end carries the new term ([[Patterns - Moving material to the end]]).
2. Add the explicit info (e.g. "When a muscle contracts, it uses calcium").
3. Metadiscourse is allowed to hold the term back: *We term these cells* **lymphokine-activated killer (LAK) cells**.

## Examples
> ✗ The role of calcium blockers in the control of cardiac irregularity can be seen through an understanding of the role of calcium in the activation of muscle cells. The regulatory proteins actin, myosin, ... make up the sarcomere.
> ✓ When a muscle contracts, it uses calcium. If we can understand how calcium affects muscle contraction, we can explain how the drugs called "calcium blockers" control cardiac irregularity. The basic unit of muscle contraction is the sarcomere.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Works even for professional readers; no exception stated.
