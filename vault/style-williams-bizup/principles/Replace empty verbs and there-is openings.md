# Replace empty verbs and there-is openings

**Rule:** When the verb is `be, seem, have, make, conduct, do` or the sentence opens `There is/are` with a nominalization after it, discard the empty frame: verbify the nominalization, drop the light verb, and put the character in the subject.
**Why (reader effect):** Light verbs carry no meaning, so the real content sits in nouns; the sentence looks complete but tells no story.

## Diagnose
- Subject = nominalization + verb `is/was/has` (pattern 1).
- Light verb + nominalization object: `conducted an investigation`, `did a review`, `make a reduction` (pattern 2).
- `There is/are/was` + nominalization: `There is no need for our further study` (pattern 4).

## Revise
1. Nominalization -> verb (`investigation -> investigate`).
2. Replace the empty verb with it (`conducted -> investigated`) when the nominalization followed it.
3. For `There is` + nominalization, find the character and restore: `We need not study...`.

## Examples
> ✗ There is no need for our further study of this problem.
> ✓ We need not study this problem further.

> ✗ The agency conducted an investigation into the matter.
> ✓ The agency investigated the matter.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- `There is` is fine for plain existence claims with no hidden action. (Implied; source targets only nominalizations after it.)
