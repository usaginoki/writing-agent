# Thread themes through a passage

**Rule:** Repeat key concepts through the passage: character-concepts as topics (subjects), other concepts as themes in nouns, verbs, adjectives.
**Why (reader effect):** readers understand ideas more easily when they can connect them to a few key concepts; consistent topics plus repeated themes plus a stressed opening sentence make a passage "hang together".

## Diagnose
- Highlight related words; do 2-3 clusters recur across sentences? If sentence topics are random, fix topics first.

## Revise
1. Make subjects consistent (*researchers/physicians*, *diagnosis/tests*).
2. Weave theme words through (tests: *diagnosis, guess, new tests, predict*; new problem: *risk, tragedy, devastate*).
3. Stress them in the opening ([[Announce key themes in the stress of the opening sentence]]).

## Examples
> ✗ Genetic clues have become the basis of newer and more reliable tests in the last few years, however. The risk of human tragedy ... has resulted from ... (random openers)
> ✓ In the past few years, however, physicians have been able to use new and more reliable tests focusing on genetic clues. But in the accuracy of these new tests lies the risk of another kind of human tragedy...
