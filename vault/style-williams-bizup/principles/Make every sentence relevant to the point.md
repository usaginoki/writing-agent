# Make every sentence relevant to the point

**Rule:** Every sentence must visibly bear on the point of the unit it belongs to. Relevant material: background/context; points of sections and whole; reasons supporting a point; evidence/facts/data supporting a reason; explanation of reasoning or methods; consideration of other views.
**Why (reader effect):** when readers can't see how a sentence relates to the point, they judge the passage incoherent. (No simple rule of relevance exists; the list is the guide.)

## Diagnose
- For each sentence ask which category above it serves. None? Suspect.
- See [[Relevance test]].

## Revise
1. Delete sentences that serve no category.
2. If a stray fact matters, link it explicitly to the point or move it.

## Examples
> ✗ In the fact/opinion study passage: "These essays were also badly organized in several ways." "They also produced fewer spelling and punctuation errors." "Their last essay was significantly longer..."
> ✓ Remove them; keep only material about distinguishing fact from opinion.
