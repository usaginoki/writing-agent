# Use clarity honestly rather than abandoning it

**Rule:** Accept that clarity is an ideological, socially created value, but do not conclude that clear writing is itself oppressive. The threat is the unethical use of clarity (as with science), not clarity.
**Why (reader effect):** Language does carry power (Latin, French, Latinate nominalizations, Standard English as gatekeepers), yet clarity lets readers hold those in power to account. Clarity is hard and unnatural, society must work to keep it.

## Diagnose
- Is someone arguing that plain writing "dumbs down" political complexity? Check whether the clear version actually drops content.
- Ask whether clarity serves truth-telling or deception.

## Revise
1. Keep complexity of content; clarify the sentences.
2. Insist that those who manage affairs tell the truth as clearly as they can; call them out when they don't.

## Examples
> ✗ "Clear language is a tool of the dominant culture."
> ✓ Clarity can be abused like science; abuse, not clarity, is the problem.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- The critics' point that language is bound up with politics and exclusion is "one good point."
