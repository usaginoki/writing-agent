# Repeat key themes in the body

**Rule:** In the body of the document or section, repeat the themes announced in the point sentence regularly, in the same terms, and keep topics consistent.
**Why (reader effect):** readers look for the announced concepts to organize the whole; if the terms vanish or change, they lose the thread.

## Diagnose
- Highlight key terms from the point sentence; count occurrences through the body (see [[Key-term recurrence test]]).
- Look for synonyms that disguise the same concept.

## Revise
1. Reuse the exact key terms in topic/stress positions.
2. Keep subjects consistent ([[Topic String]]) and follow old-before-new ([[Old-New Information]]).

## Examples
> ✓ Alzheimer's model paragraph: opening point names *early and accurate diagnosis*, *informing*, *human problem*; the following segment repeats *diagnoses, accuracy, predict, early diagnosis, human tragedy*.
