# End with of plus a weighty word

**Rule:** Close a sentence with an *of* phrase whose object is a stressed word: the light *of the* quickens the rhythm just before the climactic noun.
**Why (reader effect):** The unstressed run-up makes the final stressed syllable land harder. It marks self-conscious elegance (Gibbon: *the fairest part of the earth*).

## Diagnose
- Look for closing noun phrases that are flat pre-modified strings (*executive governmental powers*, *the earth's fairest, most civilized part*).

## Revise
1. Unpack the string into head noun + *of* + weighty word.
2. Keep the strongest, monosyllabic or heavy word last (old, mankind, government, esteem).
3. Pair several such phrases across a passage for rhythm.

## Examples
> ✗ ...all executive governmental powers.
> ✓ ...all the executive powers of government. (Gibbon)
> ✗ ...the earth's fairest, most civilized part.
> ✓ ...the fairest part of the earth, and the most civilized portion of mankind.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Chains of *of* phrases elsewhere become nominalization sprawl; use this at endings only.
- Flavor of formal, self-conscious style; wrong for plain reports.
