# Trim the end of the sentence

**Rule:** If the tail repeats what an earlier word already means, cut it so the real point ends the sentence.
**Why (reader effect):** redundant tails occupy the stress position with nothing.

## Diagnose
- At the end, does a phrase merely restate (e.g. *social behavior* = *the way we act in situations*)?

## Revise
1. Cut everything after the word that says it.

## Examples
> ✗ Sociobiologists claim that our genes control our social behavior in the way we act in situations we are in every day.
> ✓ Sociobiologists claim that our genes control our social behavior.

## When not to apply / exceptions
- Cut only if truly redundant; Ex. 6.4 asks whether your reader trimmed material you thought necessary.
