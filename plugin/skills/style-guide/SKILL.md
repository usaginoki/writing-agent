## Five things to hold in mind at all times
1. Readers feel clarity when the **characters are subjects** and the **actions are verbs**.
2. **Old before new, short before long, point before detail**, at every level from sentence to document.
3. A unit's **point** goes at the end of its short opening segment, and its **key terms** recur in the body.
4. **Concise ≠ terse.** Cut what readers don't need, but keep hedges that carry real uncertainty, and keep signposts that long documents need.
5. These are **revision tools**. Diagnose first, then revise, and never apply a rule where its "leave it when" applies.
